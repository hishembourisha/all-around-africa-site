About the channel

All Around Africa is a YouTube channel that explains Africa through facts: its countries and cities, its history, its economy, its people and cultures. The videos summarise publicly available sources such as news reports, statistics from international organisations and freely accessible articles.

How the videos are made

Production is largely automated. Self-developed software collects sources, ranks topics, writes a script, checks the script's statements against the sources, generates narration and imagery and assembles the video. The finished video is then uploaded to the operator's own YouTube channel.

Note on AI-generated content: The narration voice and the imagery of these videos are generated synthetically. Every video is labelled on YouTube as containing altered or synthetic content. The statements are based on the cited sources and are cross-checked in the script; accuracy and completeness are nevertheless not guaranteed.

The application behind the channel

For uploading, the software uses the YouTube Data API. The registered application is called All Around Africa Pipeline. It is a private tool of the operator and is not offered to anyone else: it signs in only to the operator's own YouTube channel, uploads videos there, sets their thumbnails and metadata and posts a comment under its own videos. Which data is processed is described in the Privacy Policy.
